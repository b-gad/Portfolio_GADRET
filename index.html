Manufacturing Processes · Foundry

Sand Casting of an AlSi7 Part

Complete sand-casting cycle of an AlSi7 aluminium part, from design calculations to inspection of the casting. Sized the riser using the thermal modulus method and the gating system from the flow-rate equation, then built the two-part sand mould with resin-bonded cores and poured the part at about 765 °C. Inspection showed no shrinkage cavity, which validates the riser design; the 4.3 s pouring time (3 s targeted) and the 1.9 % linear shrinkage (1.25 % expected) were analysed and explained. Improvement paths: numerical simulation and exothermic risers.
